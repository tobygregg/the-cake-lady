Celebration cakes

Themed cakes for birthdays and milestones, from a toolbox for a fourth birthday to a wheel of cheese for a 60th. Tell me who it’s for and what they love, and I’ll take it from there.

What’s included

  • Hand-modelled sugar figures and details
  • Names, ages and messages
  • A decorated board to finish the theme
  • Designs for baby showers and new arrivals

Cupcakes

Swirled buttercream cupcakes, finished to match your party or simply pretty. Order a box to share, or have them boxed one by one as party favours.

What’s included

  • Printed edible toppers to match a theme

Cakes

Photo cakes

A favourite photo or picture printed onto an edible topper and set on a smooth iced cake. A simple way to make it personal for a big birthday.

What’s included

  • Your photo or picture, printed edible
  • A message on the cake and the board
  • Iced sides and details to match the picture
  • Matching printed cupcakes if you’d like them

How ordering works

Most orders start with a date and an idea. Here’s how it goes from there.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call, email or send the form with your date, the occasion and roughly how many it needs to feed.

  2. 2

    Plan the design

    We talk through the theme, colours, names and any pictures you’d like on it.

  3. 3

    Baked and decorated

    Your cake is baked and decorated in time for your date.

  4. 4

    Boxed and ready

    Everything comes boxed up and ready for the celebration.

Hello, I’m Bryony

I make decorated cakes and cupcakes for all occasions here in Guernsey. Most orders start with a theme, whether that’s a favourite hobby, a big birthday or a new arrival, and I design each cake around it.

The sugar figures, letters and little details are all made by hand, so no two cakes are quite the same.
